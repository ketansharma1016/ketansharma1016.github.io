About me

Hello, I’m Ketan Sharma, a mechanical engineering student at San Jose State University, graduating in May 2028. I’m interested in mechanical design, manufacturing and the mechatronic systems that make products work reliably.

Through coursework in mechatronics, machine operations and safety and machining processes, I’ve modeled parts in SolidWorks with GD&T and DFM in mind, then built them myself: machining aluminum on a lathe and mill, forming sheet metal, woodworking, 3D printing and wiring microcontroller circuits.

I’m looking for a mechanical design internship in Silicon Valley, where I can work on real parts and learn how designs get built and tested. My two years as a technology consultant at Target also taught me to explain technical problems clearly.
